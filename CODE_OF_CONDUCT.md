# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of **Project Freedom**, pledge to make participation in our community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Our goal is to foster an open, inclusive, and collaborative environment where everyone feels safe to contribute.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate.
* Welcoming new contributors.
* Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
* Focusing on what is best for the project.
* Showing empathy toward other community members.
* Helping others learn and improve.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
* Personal attacks or insulting language.
* Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Spam, abusive comments, or intimidation.
* Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

---

# Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

* Clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
* Reviewing and responding to reports fairly and promptly.
* Taking appropriate action when unacceptable behavior occurs.
* Maintaining a respectful and productive community.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, pull requests, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Project Freedom community spaces, including but not limited to:

* GitHub Issues
* Pull Requests
* Discussions
* Documentation
* Project Wiki
* Community chats
* Any other official communication channels

It also applies when an individual is officially representing Project Freedom in public spaces.

# Enforcement

Community leaders will review every report carefully and determine an appropriate response based on the severity of the incident.

Possible actions include:

* Friendly reminder
* Official warning
* Temporary restriction
* Permanent ban from the community

Enforcement decisions will be made fairly and consistently.
